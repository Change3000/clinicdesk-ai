ClinicDesk AI

Triage for a GP clinic's patient inbox: flags emergencies, sorts every message to the right team, pulls out the key details and drafts a reply for staff to approve.

Runs entirely in your browser. No message leaves this device.

Sample inbox: 24 example messages written for this demo for a fictional clinic (Harbourside Family Practice). No real patient data.

Paste or type a patient message

Try SMS shorthand, typos, or something urgent. Results update as you type.

Batch triage

One message per line (for example, copied from an email export or SMS log). Or load a CSV with a column named message.

How it works

Every message goes through four steps. Safety comes first and always wins over the model.

1. Emergency checkRed-flag rules (chest pain, breathing, stroke signs, anaphylaxis, suicide risk, overdose, collapse, bleeding, seizures, unwell baby) plus a learned urgency model. Either one firing stops automation: no drafted reply, call the patient now.
2. Intent modelTF-IDF + logistic regression trained in Python (scikit-learn) on 315 example messages across 9 request types, exported to JSON and run here in JavaScript.
3. Detail extractionPulls out who it's for, doctor, medicines, tests, specialist, dates and times, phone number, and masks Medicare numbers.
4. Route and draftSends it to the right team with a suggested response time and drafts a reply. Staff approve every reply; low-confidence messages go to a person.

Limits

Trained on synthetic messages written for this project, so accuracy on a real clinic's inbox will be lower until it is retrained on de-identified real messages. English only. It is an admin tool, not a medical device: it never gives clinical advice, and every drafted reply needs a staff member to approve it. The emergency check prefers false alarms to misses, and it still misses some wording, so it supports reception staff rather than replacing them.
